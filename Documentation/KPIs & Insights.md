
# Services Company Analysis

## KPIs & Business Insights

### 1. Executive Overview
- Total Revenue
- Total Services
- Total Clients
- Total Hours
- Average Revenue per Service
- Average Revenue per Hour
- Average Hourly Rate

### 2. Service & Department Performance
- Revenue by Service Type
- Revenue by Department
- Services Count by Department
- Total Hours by Department
- Top Performing Services
- Top Performing Departments

### 3. Client & Geographic Analysis
- Top 10 Clients by Revenue
- Revenue by Country
- Revenue by Region
- Clients by Country
- Client Revenue Ranking
- Client Revenue Share
- Top 5 Clients Revenue Contribution

### 4. Time-Based Analysis
- Monthly Revenue Trend
- Month-over-Month (MoM) Revenue Growth
- Year-over-Year (YoY) Revenue Growth
- Revenue Performance Over Time

### 5. Business Objectives
- Evaluate overall business revenue and service performance.
- Identify the highest revenue-generating services.
- Compare department performance.
- Identify the most valuable clients.
- Analyze revenue distribution across countries and regions.
- Monitor monthly and yearly revenue trends.
- Support data-driven business decisions.
